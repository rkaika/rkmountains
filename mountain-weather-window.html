Interpretation Notes

Baseline

NWS first

Use the NWS hourly data here as the official, human-edited baseline. For raw model maps, compare Windy layers at the same time, layer, and altitude.

Close-in

HRRR inside 48 hours

For a 1 am start, HRRR is usually the lead model for shower timing, wind shifts, and cloud detail once you are within about two days.

Cross-check

NAM 3 km for context

If HRRR and NAM agree that snow has ended and winds are manageable, confidence improves. If they disagree, make the plan more conservative.

Coordinates are precomputed route references, not official navigation data. NWS grid terrain is smoothed, so summit ridges can be colder, windier, or cloudier than the forecast grid cell.
